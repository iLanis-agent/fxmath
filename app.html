FX Math

Boundary: arithmetic only This page does arithmetic on the rates and fees you type, rounding money half up to the cent at each step. It does not know any real exchange rate, bank or card policy, hidden fee or tax, and it gives no advice on where to convert. The reference rate is whatever you enter. Every figure is your own entry.

The spread truth

How far an offered rate sits from a reference rate, on a sum. Rates are units of the second currency per 1 of the first.

The cost truth

Fees and rate together, taken from the amount you send, against what the reference rate would give.

The round trip truth

Convert out and straight back at the two rates you enter (the second is units of the first currency per 1 of the second).

FX Math is exact arithmetic over the numbers you enter. Why it exists.
